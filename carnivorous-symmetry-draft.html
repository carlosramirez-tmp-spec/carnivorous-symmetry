Trap Symmetry in Carnivorous Plants: A Mathematical Analysis

Abstract

This paper examines bilateral and radial symmetry patterns in the trap structures of Nepenthes, Dionaea muscipula, and Utricularia species using geometric group theory. Findings suggest that trap efficiency correlates with symmetry order in a non-linear fashion, with peak efficiency at D4 symmetry groups.

Findings

Analysis of 47 trap specimens across three genera revealed consistent dihedral symmetry patterns. SVG diagrams illustrating symmetry axes are included in the supplementary materials.
